# Sample 12-Slide Page Plan

Synthetic example for a fictional manufacturing turnaround case.

| Page | Section | Slide title | Management question | Primary proof object | Component roles |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover | Fictional Components Co. has moved from growth mode to recovery mode | What is the overall message? | Title system + KPI strip | title_header, kpi_strip |
| 2 | Executive Summary | The business should keep phase one, stop phase two, and prioritize cash-safe recovery | What should leadership decide? | Executive dashboard | kpi_strip, comparison_table, callout |
| 3 | Diagnosis | Losses are driven by market reset, cost structure, sales model, and expansion timing | Why did the project underperform? | Four-driver diagnostic | issue_tree, causal_chain |
| 4 | Investment Review | Phase-one assets exist, but phase-two investment would add risk before the core line stabilizes | Should expansion continue? | Timeline + investment ledger | timeline, ledger_table |
| 5 | Market Reset | Selling price has fallen far below the original plan, invalidating the expansion revenue model | What changed externally? | Price slope chart | chart_frame, metric_card |
| 6 | Utilization | Effective utilization remains too low to absorb fixed costs | Why does unit cost stay high? | Utilization bridge | bridge_chart, comparison_table |
| 7 | Financial Overview | Cash flow has recovered before earnings, but net profit remains below the recovery line | Is the business fixed yet? | Profit/cash comparison | kpi_strip, chart_frame |
| 8 | Cost Structure | Cost disadvantage is multi-point, not a single purchasing problem | Where should management act? | Cost layer model | hierarchy_layer, comparison_table |
| 9 | Sales Model | Cash-safe sales reduce receivable risk but limit volume in a credit-driven market | What constrains sales recovery? | Sales model comparison | comparison_table, callout |
| 10 | Customer Strategy | Orders should be screened by unit contribution and collection quality | Which orders should be accepted? | 2x2 customer matrix | decision_matrix |
| 11 | Recovery Roadmap | The next 12 months should move from cash protection to stable contribution and selective growth | How should recovery be sequenced? | Roadmap + action table | roadmap, ledger_table |
| 12 | Decision Page | Leadership should approve phase-two stop, asset monetization, and controlled sales-policy pilots | What approvals are required? | Decision table | comparison_table, callout |

## Layout Notes

- No fixed bottom insight box is used.
- Chapter-like pages still include evidence and output notes.
- Similar proof objects are separated to avoid thumbnail monotony.
- The contact sheet should show clear variation: dashboard, diagnostic, timeline, slope, bridge, matrix, roadmap, decision table.
