# Turnaround Plan Example

This is a sanitized, fictional example showing how Consulting Slide Master turns messy operating materials into an executive turnaround deck.

No real company, client, source file, template, or financial data is included.

## Scenario

Fictional case:

> Fictional Components Co. is a manufacturing business unit that expanded capacity during a high-demand cycle. After market prices fell and utilization dropped, the business moved from growth mode to recovery mode. Leadership needs a board-ready deck to decide whether to keep the current production line, stop the second-phase expansion, and approve a 12-month operating improvement plan.

## Input Materials

Synthetic sources:

- investment approval memo
- market price notes
- monthly operating data
- cost breakdown spreadsheet
- sales channel interview notes
- asset status ledger
- management action list
