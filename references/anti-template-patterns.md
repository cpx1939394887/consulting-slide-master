# Anti-Template Patterns

Avoid these patterns unless there is a strong content reason.

## Repetitive Three-Card Page

Three cards can be useful once. Repeating them across the deck makes the output look autogenerated.

## Fixed Bottom "So What" Box

Do not force every page into a bottom insight box. Management implication should appear where the content naturally needs it.

## Empty Chapter Divider

Chapter pages must contain useful information, not just a large title.

## Decorative Chart

If a chart does not prove the slide title, remove or replace it.

## Appendix Table As Main Slide

A main slide table must have interpretation, highlighting, and a management purpose.

## Template Over Content

Never delete important evidence merely to fit a template. Redesign the layout instead.

## Thumbnail Monotony

If the contact sheet shows many pages with the same rhythm, revise layout variety.

## Sparse Faux Premium

Large whitespace is not automatically premium. In executive business decks, sparse pages often look unfinished.

## Descriptive Titles

Avoid titles such as "Market Analysis", "Financial Situation", "Cost Analysis", or "Next Steps". Use conclusion sentences.
